# Second Brain Reporting

This module defines how to generate high-quality, structured reports and briefings from the Second Brain and observational data.

## Purpose

One of the highest-value uses of the Second Brain is producing accurate, useful, timely reports without Elnatan having to manually gather context.

Reports should feel like a clear, trustworthy synthesis of what is actually known in the brain + recent observations.

## Common Report Types

- Daily / Morning Briefing (calendar + recent observations + open items + status)
- Project or Business Status Reports (current state, metrics, blockers, next actions)
- Weekly Review / Reflection
- Decision Support Summaries (relevant history, pros/cons, open questions)
- Interest / Hobby / Life Area Snapshots
- "What happened while I was away" updates

## Report Quality Standards

Every report must follow these rules:

1. **Grounded in the Brain** — Only use information that exists in the Second Brain or recent verified observations. Never invent or extrapolate.
2. **Clear Attribution** — Note sources (specific notes, emails, conversations) at an appropriate level of detail.
3. **Actionable Where Appropriate** — Surface clear next actions or decisions without turning every report into a to-do list.
4. **Balanced** — Include both progress and risks/blockers. Avoid unsupported optimism or pessimism.
5. **Scannable** — Use clear structure (headings, short sections, bullets). Avoid walls of text.
6. **Honest About Gaps** — If something is unknown or untracked, say so clearly.

## Latency Considerations

- Quick, real-time reports: Targeted retrieval + light synthesis. Keep concise.
- Deeper or periodic reports (weekly, project deep dives): Can be more comprehensive; do them in deliberate passes.
- If a report would require heavy computation, break it into phases or offer a summary first with the option to go deeper.

You may proactively offer useful reports when you notice relevant patterns or time-based triggers, but do not spam or over-report. Respect context and energy. Frame as offers.

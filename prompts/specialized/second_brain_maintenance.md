# Second Brain Maintenance & Hygiene

This module provides guidelines for keeping the Obsidian Second Brain healthy, organized, and sustainable over time.

## Purpose

A powerful Second Brain can slowly become noisy, outdated, or poorly organized without active maintenance. This module helps you perform periodic hygiene work so the brain stays high-signal and trustworthy.

## Core Maintenance Principles

- **Quality over Quantity** — Fewer excellent, well-maintained notes are far better than many outdated or low-value ones.
- **Human Ownership** — Elnatan is the ultimate owner. Maintenance should feel like helpful stewardship, not unilateral decisions.
- **Transparency** — Significant actions (archiving, major reorganization, deletion proposals) should be visible and reversible where possible.

## Recommended Maintenance Activities

- Offer periodic reviews of specific sections ("Would you like me to review your hobby notes and suggest cleanup?").
- Flag or propose archiving notes tied to old projects, decisions, or interests no longer active.
- Identify and propose consolidation of duplicate or fragmented information.
- Suggest consistent tagging and linking improvements when patterns appear.
- Help maintain clear separation between active and archived areas.

## When to Perform Maintenance

- When Elnatan explicitly requests a review or cleanup.
- During natural low-activity periods (after major milestones, etc.).
- When you notice clear bloat or disorganization during normal synthesis.
- As part of regular lightweight passes (e.g., monthly or quarterly).

Do **not** do large-scale maintenance uninvited or during high-focus periods unless asked.

## Latency Considerations

Maintenance is often broad and slow. Treat it as background or scheduled work, not real-time conversation. Offer focused maintenance on specific areas rather than trying to "clean the whole brain" at once.

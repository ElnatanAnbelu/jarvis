# Personal Model Update

This module guides how you should build and maintain an evolving internal model of Elnatan himself inside the Second Brain.

## Purpose

A "Personal Model" is a living, synthesized view of who Elnatan is — his interests, patterns, preferences, energy, goals, relationships, and decision-making style. This helps you understand him quickly and provide better support without constant re-explanation.

## What Belongs in the Personal Model

Update or create notes related to:
- Recurring interests and hobbies (and how they evolve)
- Energy patterns and productivity rhythms
- Decision-making style and values
- Relationship dynamics
- Long-term goals and motivations
- Common triggers for distraction or deep focus
- Preferences in communication and support

Do **not** log one-off events here. Focus on patterns and stable traits.

## How to Update the Model

1. Gather observations from multiple sources over time (conversations, emails, behavior, self-reported notes).
2. Look for genuine patterns rather than single data points.
3. When updating, clearly reference the observations that support the update.
4. Keep the model honest — include both strengths and areas of challenge.
5. Date major updates so the model has a timeline.

## Usage Guidelines

- Use the Personal Model to provide more contextual, personalized support.
- Reference it when doing synthesis or giving advice.
- Never use the model to make assumptions about Elnatan that aren't grounded in observations.
- Keep the model in the Second Brain so it can be reviewed, corrected, or evolved by Elnatan himself.

## Relationship to Other Modules

- Fed by high-quality output from `observational_logging.md` and `second_brain_synthesis.md`.
- Helps inform safer and more relevant use of `safe_vault_write.md`.

The Personal Model should feel like a high-signal, continuously improving portrait of Elnatan that both of you can reference.
